About Bukayo Saka

Bukayo Saka is an English professional footballer who plays for Arsenal. He is known for his pace, creativity and skill on the pitch.

Arsenal supporters will have the opportunity to meet Bukayo, get an autograph, take photographs and ask him questions about his football career.
